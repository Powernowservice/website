# Design System

## Theme

An equipment-bay interface translated into a confident service brand: white technical documentation surfaces, near-black electrical cabinets, Power Now blue, steel gray, restrained green status signals, and amber fault/heat accents.

## Color

- Background: `oklch(1 0 0)`
- Ink: `oklch(0.17 0.025 255)`
- Brand blue: `oklch(0.50 0.24 265)`
- Brand blue dark: `oklch(0.34 0.17 265)`
- Steel: `oklch(0.62 0.018 250)`
- Surface: `oklch(0.96 0.008 250)`
- Signal amber: `oklch(0.72 0.17 62)`
- Online green: `oklch(0.52 0.13 150)`
- Equipment black: `oklch(0.11 0 0)`

## Typography

Barlow Condensed carries headlines and labels with industrial compression. Source Sans 3 carries body copy, controls, and long-form content. Headlines use balanced wrapping and never exceed 88px.

## Layout

Use a 1200px content rail, full-width dark equipment sections, compact utility bands, asymmetric photo compositions, and deliberate vertical rhythm. Images are framed to preserve the equipment rather than cropped for decorative uniformity.

## Components

- Two-action header and mobile dock: Call and Book an Estimate.
- Circuit line: animated SVG current path with a static reduced-motion state.
- Evidence rail: captioned real job photography with explicit failure or service context.
- Service index: ruled editorial rows, not repeated icon cards.
- Answer block: 40-80 word direct answer at the beginning of service and article pages.
- FAQ: native details/summary disclosure with strong keyboard behavior.

## Motion

Use one orchestrated hero power-up, subtle in-view media reveals, fan rotation, and current-path dashes. Never hide content pending JavaScript. Reduced motion removes looping and transforms while keeping all information visible.
