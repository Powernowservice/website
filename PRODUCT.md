# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

North Houston homeowners, facility managers, commercial operators, generator contractors, and industrial teams who need clear evidence that an electrical service company understands load banks, PCS gear, generators, and everyday electrical faults.

## Product Purpose

Make Power Now Service the clearest local choice for load bank repair while providing direct paths to residential and commercial electrical help. Success means visitors immediately understand the work, see real field evidence, and can call or book an estimate without friction.

## Brand Personality

Field-tested, precise, mechanical.

## Anti-references

No generic contractor template, fake review counter, stock electrician photography, glassmorphism, hype copy, artificial urgency, or endless identical cards. Do not imitate editorial magazines or dark SaaS dashboards.

## Design Principles

- Show the equipment before explaining the expertise.
- Make load bank repair unmistakably primary.
- Put proof beside the claim it supports.
- Use motion to explain power flow, not decorate empty space.
- Keep the next action visible and specific.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-first navigation, clear focus states, useful alt text, 44px touch targets, high text contrast, and complete reduced-motion support.
